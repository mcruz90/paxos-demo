## Indications

<!-- cite: Overview, p. 1 -->

Livdelzi™ (seladelpar capsules – Gilead), a PPAR-delta agonist, is indicated for treatment of **primary biliary cholangitis (PBC)** in adults:
- In **combination with UDCA** for patients with inadequate response to UDCA, OR
- As **monotherapy** for patients unable to tolerate UDCA

Approved under accelerated approval based on ALP reduction; improvement in survival or liver decompensation events has not yet been established.

**Limitation of use:** Not recommended in patients who have or develop decompensated cirrhosis (ascites, variceal bleeding, hepatic encephalopathy).

## Diagnostic Criteria

<!-- cite: Policy Statement §1.A.ii, p. 2 -->

- Patient must be at least 18 years old and have a confirmed PBC diagnosis, defined as meeting **at least two** of the following three criteria (AASLD 2018/2021 guidelines):

1. Elevated alkaline phosphatase above the upper limit of normal (ULN) per laboratory reference values
2. Positive anti-mitochondrial antibodies (AMA), or — if AMA-negative — PBC-specific auto-antibodies (sp100 or gp210)
3. Histologic evidence of primary biliary cholangitis from a liver biopsy

Note: A biopsy is not required if the other two criteria are met.

**Specialist prescriber:** Livdelzi must be prescribed by or in consultation with a **gastroenterologist, hepatologist, or liver transplant physician**. <!-- cite: Policy Statement §1.A.v, p. 2 -->

## Biomarker Thresholds

<!-- cite: Policy Statement §1.A.ii.a, p. 2; Continuation §1.B.ii note, p. 3 -->

**Initial authorization:** ALP elevated above ULN (no specific multiplier threshold stated; elevated per lab reference values is sufficient).

**Continuation authorization (response markers):** Prescriber-determined clinical response, with examples including improvement in ALP, bilirubin, gamma-glutamyl transpeptidase (GGT), AST, and/or ALT.

## Step Therapy

<!-- cite: Policy Statement §1.A.iii, pp. 2–3 -->

Patient must meet ONE of:

**a.** Receiving ursodiol therapy for **≥12 months** with inadequate response per prescriber attestation

**OR**

**b.** Patient is **unable to tolerate** ursodiol therapy per prescriber attestation

Examples of ursodiol therapy: ursodiol generic tablets/capsules, Urso 250, Urso Forte, Actigall.

## Safety Exclusions

<!-- cite: Policy Statement §1.A.iv and §1.B.i, pp. 2–3; Conditions Not Covered §1–3, p. 3 -->

**Decompensation exclusion (absolute — both initial and renewal):**
Patient must **not** currently have, or have a **history** of, any hepatic decompensation event. Examples: ascites, gastroesophageal varices, variceal bleeding, hepatic encephalopathy, coagulopathy.

**Hepatic impairment:** Consider discontinuing if patient progresses to moderate or severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh B or C).

**Conditions not covered:**
1. Alcoholic liver disease
2. MASLD/NAFLD/MASH/NASH (insufficient data; seladelpar MASH program halted after biopsy signals of interface hepatitis)
3. Concomitant use with Ocaliva (obeticholic acid) or Iqirvo (elafibranor) — no supporting data for combination use

## Quantity Limits

<!-- cite: Policy Statement — not specified -->

Not stated in this policy.

## Renewal Criteria

<!-- cite: Policy Statement §1.B, p. 3 -->

**Continuation authorization (12 months)** requires BOTH:

1. Patient does **not** currently have, or have a history of, a hepatic decompensation event
2. Patient has demonstrated **a response to therapy** as determined by the prescriber (improved biochemical markers: ALP, bilirubin, GGT, AST, ALT)

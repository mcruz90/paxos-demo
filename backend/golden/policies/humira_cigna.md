## Indications

<!-- cite: Overview, p. 2 -->

Adalimumab products are tumor necrosis factor inhibitors (TNFis). For **rheumatoid arthritis (RA)**, adalimumab is indicated — **alone or in combination with methotrexate or other non-biologic DMARDs** — to reduce the signs and symptoms, induce major clinical response, inhibit the progression of structural damage, and improve physical function in adults with **moderately to severely active** disease.

This anchor authors the **Rheumatoid Arthritis** indication (Policy Statement §7) of Cigna Drug Coverage Policy **IP0652 — Inflammatory Conditions – Adalimumab Products Prior Authorization Policy** (effective 05/01/2026). The policy governs brand Humira® together with all adalimumab biosimilars.

## Diagnostic Criteria — Rheumatoid Arthritis (Initial Therapy)

<!-- cite: Policy Statement §7.A, pp. 8-9 -->

Initial therapy is approved for **6 months** if the patient meets **ALL** of the following (i, ii, and iii):

1. **Age.** Patient is **≥ 18 years of age**. <!-- cite: §7.A.i, p. 8 -->
2. **Conventional synthetic DMARD trial.** Patient **has tried ONE conventional synthetic disease-modifying antirheumatic drug (DMARD) for at least 3 months**. <!-- cite: §7.A.ii, p. 8 -->
   - Examples: methotrexate (oral or injectable), leflunomide, hydroxychloroquine, sulfasalazine.
   - **Exception:** the requirement is waived if the patient has already had a 3-month trial with at least one biologic other than the requested medication. *A biosimilar of the requested biologic does not count.* A patient who has already tried a biologic for RA is **not** required to "step back" and try a conventional synthetic DMARD.
3. **Specialist prescriber.** The medication is **prescribed by or in consultation with a rheumatologist**. <!-- cite: §7.A.iii, p. 9 -->

Continuation authorization (§7.B) approves for **1 year** when the patient has been **established on therapy ≥ 6 months** AND shows a beneficial clinical response by at least one objective measure (e.g., CDAI, DAS-28, PAS-II, RAPID-3, SDAI) or symptom improvement. <!-- cite: §7.B, p. 9 -->

## Step Therapy — Preferred-Product Requirement (Non-Preferred Adalimumab)

<!-- cite: Policy Statement, NOTE, p. 5 -->

> **NOTE: This product also requires the use of preferred products before approval of a requested non-preferred adalimumab product.** Refer to the respective *Inflammatory Conditions – Adalimumab Products Preferred Specialty Management Policy* for Legacy Drug List Plans (**PSM003**), for Standard/Performance, Value/Advantage and Total Savings Prescription Drug Lists (**PSM013**), or for Individual and Family Plans (**PSM014**) **for additional preferred product criteria requirements and exceptions.**

This is the operative step-therapy gate for a **brand Humira** request: brand Humira is a **non-preferred** adalimumab product, and coverage requires trial/use of the plan's **preferred** adalimumab products first. The detailed preferred-product criteria **and their exceptions** live in the cross-referenced PSM policy (PSM003 / PSM013 / PSM014), not in this document — an appeal asserting a preferred-product exception is adjudicated against that policy.

**Products not covered on the pharmacy and medical benefits (Employer Plans and Individual and Family Plans):** adalimumab-bwwd, adalimumab-ryvk (‑ryvk NDCs starting with 51759), Amjevita (NDCs starting with 84612), **Humira (NDCs starting with 83457)**, Hyrimoz (NDCs starting with 83457). <!-- cite: Policy Statement, not-covered list, p. 5 -->

## Safety Exclusions

<!-- cite: Conditions Not Covered §1, pp. 12-13 -->

**Concurrent use with another biologic or a targeted synthetic oral small molecule drug** is **not covered** — adalimumab should not be administered in combination with another biologic or targeted synthetic oral small molecule drug for an inflammatory condition (higher adverse-event rate; no controlled data supporting additive efficacy).

**Explicitly permitted combination:** this exclusion does **NOT** exclude the use of conventional synthetic DMARDs (e.g., methotrexate, leflunomide, hydroxychloroquine, sulfasalazine) **in combination** with adalimumab. <!-- cite: Conditions Not Covered §1 Note, p. 13 -->

**Polymyalgia rheumatica (PMR)** is not covered — EULAR/ACR guidelines (2015) strongly recommend against TNFis for PMR. <!-- cite: Conditions Not Covered §2, p. 13 -->

## Quantity Limits

<!-- cite: Policy Statement — not specified for RA -->

Not stated for the RA indication in this policy.

## Renewal Criteria

<!-- cite: Policy Statement §7.B, p. 9 -->

**Continuation (1 year)** requires the patient to have been established on an adalimumab product for **≥ 6 months** AND to demonstrate a beneficial clinical response (objective measure or symptom improvement). A patient with **< 6 months** of therapy, or restarting therapy, is reviewed under §7.A (Initial Therapy).

## Notes

<!-- cite: Policy header p. 1; Policy Statement intro p. 4; History p. 15; Appendix pp. 16-17 -->

- Policy: **Inflammatory Conditions – Adalimumab Products Prior Authorization Policy**; Cigna Coverage Policy Number **IP0652**; effective **05/01/2026**.
- Because of the specialized skills required, **initial approval requires the agent to be prescribed by or in consultation with a physician who specializes in the condition being treated** (for RA, a rheumatologist). <!-- cite: Policy Statement intro, p. 4 -->
- The Appendix groups **Adalimumab SC Products (Humira®, biosimilars)** as a single TNF-inhibitor line and lists RA alternatives incl. Enbrel® (etanercept), Cimzia®, Simponi®, Orencia®, Rituxan®, Actemra®, Kevzara®, Xeljanz®, Rinvoq®, Olumiant®. <!-- cite: Appendix, pp. 16-17 -->
- **Clinical-criteria vs. formulary step therapy:** the RA §7.A criteria above (age, one conventional synthetic DMARD ≥ 3 months, rheumatologist) are the *medical-necessity* gate and apply identically to brand Humira and biosimilars. The **preferred-product / non-preferred** requirement (p. 5 NOTE) is a separate *formulary* gate specific to brand Humira and other non-preferred products; its exception criteria are in the PSM policy.
- References cited by the policy include the Humira PI (AbbVie, December 2025) and the 2021 ACR RA treatment guideline (Fraenkel et al., Arthritis Rheumatol. 2021;73(7):1108-1123).

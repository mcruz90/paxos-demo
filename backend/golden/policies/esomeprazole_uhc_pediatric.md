## Indications

<!-- cite: Background §1, p. 1 -->

Proton Pump Inhibitors (PPIs) are FDA-approved for:
- Duodenal ulcers (including *H. pylori*-associated)
- Gastric ulcers
- Erosive esophagitis
- GERD in adults and children
- Hypersecretory conditions (e.g., Zollinger-Ellison syndrome)
- GERD-related laryngitis
- Improving Pancreatic enzyme absorption in cystic fibrosis patients *(not an FDA-approved indication)*

Agents targeted by this step therapy program:
- **Nexium® for suspension (esomeprazole)**
- Prevacid® SoluTab™ (lansoprazole ODT)
- Zegerid® for suspension (omeprazole/sodium bicarbonate).

## Diagnostic Criteria

<!-- cite: Coverage Criteria §2.A, p. 1 — not specified beyond indication -->

No specific diagnosis code or lab threshold is required. Coverage is gated on step therapy history (see below). A documented clinical indication within scope (GERD, erosive esophagitis, etc.) is implied by the program scope.

## Step Therapy

<!-- cite: Coverage Criteria §2.A.1–2, pp. 1–2 -->

### Nexium suspension / Prevacid SoluTabs — approved on ONE of:

**a. Step therapy failure** — documented history of failure, contraindication, or intolerance to **all three** of the following (reason must be provided for each):
1. A prescription formulation of omeprazole
2. Pantoprazole
3. Rabeprazole

**OR**

**b. Swallowing limitation** — patient is unable to swallow a tablet or capsule dosage form due to one of:
1. Age
2. Oral/motor difficulties
3. Dysphagia

**OR**

**c. Feeding tube** — patient administers medications via feeding tube

> **Pediatric auto-process rule:** Claims for Nexium suspension and lansoprazole ODTs (generic Prevacid SoluTabs) **will process automatically — no PA required — for patients under age 6.** Step therapy gates apply for ages 6 and older.

### Zegerid suspension (second-line only) — approved on:

**a.** History of failure, contraindication, or intolerance to **both** of:
1. Nexium suspension (esomeprazole)
2. Lansoprazole ODTs (generic Prevacid SoluTabs)

## Indications

<!-- cite: Overview, p. 1; Policy Statement / FDA-Approved Indication, p. 2 -->

Adjunct to diet and exercise to improve glycemic control in adults with **type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM)**. Selected agents carry additional labeled indications:

- **Cardiovascular risk reduction** in adults with T2DM: liraglutide (Victoza/generic), Ozempic, Rybelsus, Trulicity
- **CKD risk reduction** (reduce eGFR decline, ESKD, CV death) in adults with T2DM and chronic kidney disease: Ozempic

Agents covered by this policy: Byetta (exenatide) and generic, Mounjaro (tirzepatide), Ozempic injection and tablets (semaglutide), Rybelsus (semaglutide tablets), Trulicity (dulaglutide), Victoza (liraglutide) and generic.

## Diagnostic Criteria

<!-- cite: Policy Statement / FDA-Approved Indication §1, p. 2 -->

Confirmed diagnosis of **type 2 diabetes mellitus** is required for all agents. Age threshold varies by agent:

| Agent | Minimum age |
|---|---|
| Byetta, exenatide (generic), Ozempic injection, Ozempic tablets, Rybelsus | ≥ 18 years |
| Bydureon BCise (obsolete 03/31/2025), Mounjaro, Trulicity, Victoza, liraglutide (generic) | ≥ 10 years |

Approval duration: **1 year**.

## Biomarker Thresholds

<!-- cite: Policy Statement — not specified -->

No A1c, fasting glucose, BMI, or other lab threshold is stated in this policy. Coverage is based on confirmed T2DM diagnosis and age criterion only.

## Step Therapy

<!-- cite: Policy Statement — not specified; History / Automation notes, pp. 3–4 -->

No formal prior-therapy step requirement is stated in this policy. A documented trial of metformin or another oral antidiabetic is **not required** by the PA criteria.

Note — automated adjudication pathway (not a PA requirement, but relevant to claim routing): Claims for Mounjaro, Victoza/liraglutide, and exenatide generic will auto-adjudicate if the patient has a claim history for **one oral diabetes medication** (excluding Rybelsus/Ozempic tablets and single-entity metformin) within the past 130 days **and** meets the applicable age threshold. If either condition is not met, the PA criteria above apply instead.

## Safety Exclusions

<!-- cite: Conditions Not Covered §1–5, pp. 2–3 -->

The following uses are considered **not medically necessary** under this policy:

1. **Weight loss** — agents in this policy are not FDA-approved for overweight/obesity without T2DM. The same molecules at higher doses (Wegovy/semaglutide, Saxenda/liraglutide, Zepbound/tirzepatide) are indicated for weight management and are outside the scope of this policy.
2. **Type 1 Diabetes Mellitus** — none of the GLP-1/GIP agonists in this policy are indicated for T1DM.
3. **Prediabetes / Diabetes Prevention** — not indicated for elevated blood glucose without a T2DM diagnosis. ADA guidelines recommend metformin and lifestyle modification for prevention.
4. **Metabolic Syndrome without T2DM** — not indicated.
5. **Concomitant use with another GLP-1 or GLP-1/GIP agonist** — must not be combined with another agent in this class, including weight-management GLP-1s (Wegovy, Saxenda, Zepbound).
